The DB-shaped pain

Limited Gemini use is rational. The missing layer is control evidence.

Research teams can summarize transcripts and draft faster, but the firm still owns supervision, recordkeeping, conflicts, information barriers, and analyst accountability. A generic AI chat window does not prove that a claim was supported, a number tied to the model, an analyst actually reviewed the output, or a supervisor/compliance reviewer approved it in an auditable sequence.

ClawMoat does not replace Gemini. It wraps Gemini-assisted research with pre-publication controls and audit receipts.

What it checks

Research-specific controls, not generic AI governance vapor.

Claim support and citations

Extracts factual and numeric claims, checks approved filings, transcripts, or source packs, and requires explicit source citations on material research claims.

Model tie-out

Flags price targets, EPS, EBITDA, margins, and percentages that do not tie to the supplied model extract.

Prompt-injection source scan

Scans transcripts, PDFs, web captures, and filings before their text influences an AI workflow or tool call.

Restricted-list and MNPI gates

Compares draft/source/model text against restricted issuer and watch-list policy packs, and holds content that looks like selective disclosure or non-public information.

Analyst attestation

Detects AI-assisted content that lacks review evidence, so Reg AC-style accountability does not vanish into chat history.

Audit receipt

Exports artifact hashes, source hashes, policy pack version, control matrix, controls applied, findings, and supervisor checklist for books-and-records evidence.
