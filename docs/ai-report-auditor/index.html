Claim audit taxonomy

  • Supported: approved source evidence directly supports the claim.
  • Contradicted: approved source evidence says something different.
  • Misbound: the cited number, entity, or date appears, but is attached to the wrong metric, period, or source context.
  • Uncited: a material claim has no explicit source citation.
  • Not verifiable from provided sources: the claim may be true, but it is not proven by the supplied corpus.
  • Needs human review: the claim is ambiguous, judgmental, or outside deterministic source scope.

Workflow CLI/API + ledger + approval chain

  1. CLI/API preflight: run clawmoat research preflight or POST the draft/source/model bundle to /api/research/preflight. Output: research-preflight.json.
  2. Review packet: the preflight becomes a hash-addressed review packet with required approval roles and retained source fingerprints.
  3. Approval chain: supervisor and compliance decisions append as independent ledger events. Analyst self-approval is rejected.
  4. Tamper evidence: every event includes previousHash and entryHash; the signed head hash is retained externally. Output: supervision-ledger.jsonl and anchor.json.
  5. Enterprise handoff: retention, model-risk, SOC/vendor-risk, and readiness artifacts tell the buyer what is pilot-ready and what still blocks official use.
