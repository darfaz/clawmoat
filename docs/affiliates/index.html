❓ FAQ

When do I get paid?
Every Friday. We batch payouts weekly. Minimum payout: $10 USDT.
How long does the referral cookie last?
30 days. If someone clicks your link and signs up within 30 days, you get credit.
Do I earn on renewals?
Currently, 50% of the first month only. We may add recurring commissions for top-tier affiliates.
Can I refer enterprise customers?
Yes! Business and Enterprise intro bounties are paid manually after the deal closes. Minimum $500 for Business, $2,500 for Enterprise.
Is there a limit?
No cap. Refer 1,000 people, earn $14,500+ USDT. We want you to go wild.
What if ClawMoat is free?
Free users don't generate payouts. Payouts only trigger on paid plan signups (Developer, Team, Business, Enterprise).
